Questions

What do I get with amux Cloud?
A dedicated server that runs amux for you and nobody else, at your own address (yourname.amux.io). Your workers keep running when your laptop is closed.
Is there a free trial?
The open-source version is free forever and has every feature. amux Cloud is paid from the start, and you can cancel at any time.
Do I need my own model account or API key?
Yes. Sign in with Claude, ChatGPT or Google on your server, or paste an Anthropic, OpenAI or Gemini API key. Model usage is billed by that provider, separately from amux Cloud.
Can I change plans?
Yes, from billing on your server. A larger plan moves your server to bigger hardware within a few minutes.
What happens when I cancel?
Your server runs until the end of the period you paid for. We then save a snapshot and turn it off. The snapshot is kept for 30 days, and resubscribing restores it.
Is the self-hosted version feature-complete?
Yes. amux is fully open source. Cloud adds the server and its address; the product is the same.
