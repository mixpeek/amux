amux: your AI engineering team

What it replaces: junior engineers, freelancers or contractors you cannot afford yet. amux is a self-hosted control plane for running an AI engineering team from a single dashboard or your phone. It launches and monitors dozens of parallel Claude Code, Codex or Gemini CLI sessions, with a kanban board, notes, CRM, email, browser automation and a scheduler built in. Queue a backlog before bed and review the work in the morning.

Cost: free to self-host (MIT + Commons Clause); you pay for the agents' own subscriptions or API tokens. amux Cloud, your own always-on server at yourname.amux.io, is $39/mo, or $69/mo on Team if you run agents for several clients.

Setup: curl -fsSL https://amux.io/install.sh | bash, or start Cloud. github.com/mixpeek/amux

Claude (Anthropic)

What it replaces: research assistants, technical writers, copywriters and analysts. Use claude.ai for interactive work; a Pro or Max plan also covers Claude Code, which amux can run in parallel sessions.

Cost: free plan; Pro $20/month ($17/month billed yearly); Max from $100/month. claude.com/pricing

Cursor

What it replaces: hours of manual coding and boilerplate. Cursor is the editor for the sessions you are actively in; amux covers the ones that run while you are away.

Cost: free plan; Plus $8/month (Grok models only); Premium $30/month; Super and Ultra above that. Third-party models are billed by usage. How amux and Cursor fit together →

GitHub Copilot

What it replaces: boilerplate and inline lookup inside the editor you already use.

Cost: free plan; Pro $10/month; Pro+ $39/month; Max $100/month. Copilot plans

Notion AI

What it replaces: writing documentation, meeting summaries, specs and SOPs by hand. If your company already lives in Notion, the AI drafts inside your workspace.

Cost: included in the Business plan at $20/member/month. Free and Plus ($10/member/month) include a trial of Notion AI. notion.com/pricing

Perplexity

What it replaces: hours of searching for competitive analysis, technical research and market sizing. Perplexity returns cited answers instead of a list of links.

Cost: free plan, plus a paid Pro plan. perplexity.ai/pro

Make

What it replaces: repetitive workflows such as syncing data between tools, sending notifications and processing form submissions, built visually without code.

Cost: free tier, then paid plans priced by usage. For workflows that need judgment rather than fixed steps, an agent on the amux scheduler can do the same job.

v0 (Vercel)

What it replaces: days of UI design and frontend scaffolding. Describe a component or page and v0 generates React and Tailwind code.

Cost: free plan; Plus $30/user/month; Business $100/user/month. v0.app/pricing

Bolt.new

What it replaces: days or weeks of MVP scaffolding. Describe an app and Bolt builds a working full-stack version in the browser, which is good for validating an idea before building it properly. amux vs Bolt.new →

Cost: free plan; Pro $25/month; Teams $30/member/month. bolt.new/pricing

Linear

What it replaces: manual sprint planning, issue triage and status reports. Linear's AI features summarize issues and draft descriptions.

Cost: free plan; Basic $10/user/month; Business $16/user/month, billed yearly. linear.app/pricing

The stack we recommend

For a solo developer focused on shipping code: amux + Claude + Cursor. amux runs the agents that work while you are away, Cursor is where you write code yourself, and Claude handles analysis and writing. Self-hosting amux with Claude Pro and Cursor Premium comes to about $50/month; adding amux Cloud so the agents keep running with your laptop closed brings it to about $89. Add Perplexity and Make if research and integrations eat your week.
