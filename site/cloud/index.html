Get amux Cloud

Quick answer: amux Cloud is the open-source amux running on a dedicated server that belongs to you alone. Your workers keep going when your laptop is closed, your board, schedules and files are backed up continuously, and you reach it from any browser or the iOS app. It costs $39, $69 or $129 a month depending on the size of the server.

What you get

  • A dedicated server. Single-tenant: no other customer runs on it.
  • Your own address. yourname.amux.io, with HTTPS and sign-in.
  • Always on. Workers and schedules run around the clock.
  • Backups. The database streams to storage continuously and files are saved nightly.
  • Your models. Sign in with Claude, ChatGPT or Google, or paste an API key.
  • Teammates. Invite people by email to work on the same server.

Frequently asked questions

What is amux Cloud?

A dedicated server that runs amux for you alone, at your own address (yourname.amux.io). It is the same open-source amux, kept running, updated and backed up for you.

How much does it cost?

Solo is $39 a month, Team $69 and Max $129. Each is a dedicated server; the larger plans have more CPU, memory and disk. Billing is monthly and you can cancel at any time.

Do I need my own model account?

Yes. Sign in with Claude, ChatGPT or Google on your server, or paste an Anthropic, OpenAI or Gemini API key. Model usage is billed by that provider.

What happens if I cancel?

Your server runs until the end of the period you paid for. We then save a snapshot and turn it off. The snapshot is kept for 30 days and resubscribing restores it.
