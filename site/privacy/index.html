Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 21, 2026

Overview

amux is an open-source, local-first application. Your privacy is fundamental to how amux is designed. This policy covers the self-hosted open-source software, the amux.io website, amux Cloud, the iOS app and the amux app for ChatGPT. The security page describes what the amux team can and cannot access, in more detail.

Self-hosted amux (open source)

When you run amux on your own machine:

  • Your data stays on your machine. Session data, board items, notes and CRM contacts are stored on your local filesystem.
  • No telemetry. amux does not collect analytics, crash reports, or usage data of any kind.
  • No accounts. There is no sign-up, no login, and no user tracking.
  • No amux services. amux downloads its code from GitHub when you install or update it. Otherwise it connects only to services you set up, such as calendar sync to your own S3 bucket. The agent CLIs you run (Claude Code, Codex, Gemini CLI) talk to their own providers under those providers' terms.
  • Your code stays yours. Your source code, prompts and agent output never pass through an amux service.

amux.io website

The amux.io website is a static site hosted on GitHub Pages. It uses PostHog analytics to learn which pages lead visitors to star the GitHub repository or sign up for amux Cloud:

  • What it records: pages viewed, clicks on links to GitHub, the signup page, pricing, the walkthrough and the App Store, the site that referred you, your device type and approximate location (from your IP address), and which version of a page you saw when we test two versions.
  • A random visitor ID. PostHog stores a random ID in a cookie and in your browser's local storage so visits can be counted once. It contains no name or email address. The site also keeps your theme choice and page-test version in local storage.
  • Signup attribution. If you sign up for amux Cloud, the signup page adds that visitor ID, the page-test versions you saw and where you first came from to your Stripe order, so we can tell which pages lead to signups. The paid, server-ready and canceled steps are then recorded in PostHog under the same random ID, without your email address.
  • We do not sell this data or use it for advertising.
  • The site loads fonts from Google Fonts, which may log IP addresses under Google's privacy policy.

amux iOS app

The amux iOS app is a thin native wrapper (WKWebView) that connects to your self-hosted amux server or your amux Cloud server:

  • No data collection. The app does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data to amux or any third party.
  • Server URL only. The only data stored locally on your device is the URL of your amux server, saved in UserDefaults.
  • No analytics. The app contains no analytics SDKs, ad frameworks, or tracking code.
  • Self-signed certificates. The app accepts self-signed TLS certificates for Tailscale and local network connections to your own server.

amux Cloud

amux Cloud gives each customer their own dedicated server at yourname.amux.io, hosted by Hetzner in Germany or Finland:

  • Your data lives on your server. Workers, files, the board, notes, schedules and model keys are stored on that server only and are shared with no other customer.
  • Payment. Stripe handles payment; we never see your card number. Stripe keeps your email address, plan and billing details.
  • Your password is hashed in your browser before it is sent, and the hash is removed from the Stripe order once your server is built.
  • Your email address is your sign-in name on your server. We use it for the "server ready" email, billing links and account messages.
  • Usage counts. Our signup service reads an hourly usage report from your server, made only of counts and yes/no answers (sign-ins, connected model providers, worker, card and schedule counts, tokens used today, error counts, disk use, version). It never includes messages, prompts, names, file paths, email addresses, card titles or keys. We use it to help customers who are stuck in setup and to catch problems.
  • Our access. As the Hetzner account holder we can reach your server through Hetzner's console, rescue mode and snapshots. The security page lists everything we can technically reach.
  • We do not sell, share or monetize your data.

amux app for ChatGPT

If you connect amux to ChatGPT, the results of the tools ChatGPT calls (worker lists, worker output, board cards) are sent to OpenAI as part of your conversation and are handled under OpenAI's privacy policy. The connection passes through cloud.amux.io, which records when you connected, disconnected and called a tool, but not what the tools returned. You can disconnect at any time, and your amux stops accepting the connection at once.

Data retention

For self-hosted: you control all data. Delete it anytime from your filesystem (~/.amux/).

For amux Cloud: Hetzner keeps daily backups of your server (the last seven). If you cancel, a final snapshot is kept for 30 days so you can come back, then deleted. If you delete your account (from Settings on your server, or at amux.io/start/delete), the server, its address, its backups and its snapshots are erased right away. Stripe keeps payment records as the law requires.

Children's privacy

amux is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy
