What we automate, and what you get

  • Prospecting from public staffing dataRanks skilled nursing, home health and hospice operators by staffing turnover and leadership changes, and drafts a note built on facts they can check.
  • Database re-engagementRefreshes dormant candidates' status and matches them against open roles.
  • Job order scoringScores each new opening on salary, competition and how long it has been open, so recruiters work the ones they can fill.
  • Submittal packetsTurns a candidate record into a clean write-up for the hiring manager.
  • Guarantee-period check-insChecks in on a schedule and escalates any sign of a fall-off.
  • Quarterly client results reportsShows each client how their staffing numbers moved after they hired through you. Good for renewals and referrals.

You stay in charge

Relationships, negotiation, offers and anything touching a placed candidate's job stay with your recruiters. Every outbound email waits for your approval until you decide otherwise.

Questions

Will this send emails on its own?

Only if you want it to. Outreach starts as drafts in an approval list, and you choose what goes out.

Do we have to switch our applicant tracking system?

No. amux works with your current system through its exports or the same screens your team uses.

Which firms is this built for?

Direct-hire and permanent placement firms, especially those serving post-acute care such as skilled nursing, home health, hospice and assisted living.
