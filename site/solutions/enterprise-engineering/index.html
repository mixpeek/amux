amux for Enterprise Engineering

Scale AI coding across dozens of services and teams.

Enterprise codebases have hundreds of services. amux lets you assign agents across services for consistent upgrades, security patches, and compliance fixes — tracked on a shared board.

Local-first security

amux runs on your infrastructure. No code leaves your network. No external services. One Rust binary, SQLite and tmux.

Get started with amux

Run an AI engineering team of Claude Code, Codex and Gemini CLI agents from one dashboard or your phone. One Rust binary, MIT + Commons Clause, free to self-host on macOS or Linux.

curl -fsSL https://amux.io/install.sh | bash
# then open https://localhost:8824
