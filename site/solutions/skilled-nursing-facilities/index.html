You stay in charge

amux drafts, extracts, reminds and reports. Your MDS coordinator, DON and business office verify, sign and decide. Nothing clinical, coded or billed is ever submitted without your team's sign-off, and amux never makes a clinical or coverage decision.

Questions

Is resident data safe?

amux runs on a computer inside your building, under your own logins, so resident records and credentials stay there. When AI reads a document, it goes to a HIPAA-eligible provider under a Business Associate Agreement, and we sign a BAA before touching any protected health information.

Does this replace PointClickCare features we already use?

No. It works alongside them. amux handles the work that spans more than one system, owner reporting, and the paperwork your EHR does not finish for you.

Who should take the first call?

The administrator or owner, the person who owns census and the business office results.
