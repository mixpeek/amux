Questions

Is patient data safe?

amux runs on a computer in your practice, under your own logins. When AI reads a document, it goes to a HIPAA-eligible provider under a Business Associate Agreement, and we sign a BAA before touching any protected health information.

Does it work with our practice software?

It works with the systems your team already uses, through the same logins and screens, so your vendor does not need to offer an integration.
