Mobile Command Center

Control your entire agent fleet from your phone.

amux is a Claude Code multiplexer with a full PWA dashboard. Install it on your phone via Tailscale, walk away from your computer, and control your entire agent fleet from anywhere. Send commands, peek at live output, drag tasks on the kanban board — all from your phone. Your agents keep shipping while you're at the park with your dog.

  • Full PWA — install on iOS or Android via Tailscale
  • Send commands to any agent from your phone
  • Peek live terminal output without being at your desk
  • Drag kanban tasks between columns from your phone
