Apps

Share what your agents build. A worker builds a page and publishes it with amux publish, the link waits in the Apps tab until you approve it, and then anyone with the link can open it.

Silent clip, recorded from a real amux server with real agents, on a laptop and an iPhone-sized screen. Agent work is sped up. GIF version for docs and READMEs.

  1. Ask a worker to build something and publish it with amux publish.
  2. Everything a worker publishes waits in the Apps tab until you approve it.
  3. Approve it and the link opens for anyone who has it, no amux sign-in needed.
  4. Turn it off from the same tab whenever you want.
