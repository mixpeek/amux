Open your server

Sign in with your email and the password you chose. Then connect a model: sign in with Claude, ChatGPT or Google, or paste an API key.

Bring your local amux. On your own machine run amux export, then amux import <file> --to https://you.amux.io --user <your email>. Your cards, schedules, memories and contacts come across; keys and passwords stay behind.

We will also email you when it is ready, so you can close this page.
