Problem 1 · the one we fix most often

“The person who knew how to reconcile our payments left, and now I do it myself every weekend.”

Fixed, it looks like this

Payments are matched to invoices before Monday. You look only at the ones it could not explain.

How we fix it

  1. We sit with whoever does it today and write down every rule, including the exceptions only they know.
  2. amux signs in to your accounting system read-only, with the login kept in a vault it can use but not read.
  3. It runs on last month's files next to your team until its results match theirs.
  4. Then it runs every week. Anything it cannot explain comes to you, and nothing is posted without your approval.
  5. Every correction you make becomes a rule it follows from then on.

How long and what it costs

How long
Usually 2 to 4 weeks until it runs on its own: about a week to set it up, then side by side with your team until the results match theirs.
What it costs
$5,000 to start, including this first workflow. Then $5,000 a month for support and new workflows, plus AI usage at cost (about $200 a month). A fixed-price paid pilot is available. Pricing

Result

An outsourced accounting firm reconciles a wine importer's receivables every week. By hand it took about 8 hours. amux ran the same job unattended in 19 minutes 42 seconds.

Problem 2

“We pay for ChatGPT, but we can't let it into our real systems.”

Fixed, it looks like this

AI workers do the job inside your accounting, email and other systems, with only the access you gave them. Every action is on a record you can read.

How we fix it

  1. We pick one job that runs across the systems you already use.
  2. Each worker gets only the access that job needs, read-only to start.
  3. Passwords go in a vault. Workers sign in with them but cannot see them.
  4. Anything that leaves the company, like an email to a customer, waits for your approval on your phone.
  5. Every action is logged with who did it and who allowed it.

Problem 3

“Notes from collection calls and emails don't carry over from one day to the next.”

Fixed, it looks like this

Every call and email is logged against the account. Each morning there is a list of who needs a follow-up, with the draft ready.

How we fix it

  1. We connect the inbox and the system where your accounts live.
  2. A worker logs each contact attempt and what was said, on the account it belongs to.
  3. Each morning it lists who is due a follow-up and drafts it.
  4. You or your team approve and send.
  5. Rules for special accounts are set once and every worker follows them.

Problem 4

“The same task takes one manager 10 minutes and another 2 hours, and we can't see why.”

Fixed, it looks like this

Every recurring task has one owner, a status, and proof attached when it is done, on one board.

How we fix it

  1. We list the recurring tasks and who does each one today.
  2. Workers take the repeatable parts. Each task goes on a shared board with one owner.
  3. A task closes only when its proof is attached.
  4. You see what is done, what is waiting, and on whom.
