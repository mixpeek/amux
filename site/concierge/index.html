Dental practices

Start with insurance predetermination & eligibility

Benefits checks and predetermination packets commonly eat 10 to 20+ hours a week per location. We start there, then denial appeals, treatment letters and multi-location reporting.

Live · predetermination workflow
What we automate first
  • Pull real-time eligibility and benefits (X12 270/271) before the patient sits down.
  • Assemble payer-specific predetermination packets: radiographs, perio charting, clinical notes, and procedure codes.

Questions

Before you book

Who should take the first meeting?
The person who owns the outcome: the dental practice owner, the skilled nursing administrator, or the business owner. We want the top decision-maker on the call so we can scope the workflow that actually moves the needle.
Is my data secure?
Your logins and credentials stay on your machine inside your office. They never leave the building. When AI processes document content (a fax, a referral packet, a clinical note), it goes to a HIPAA-eligible model provider under a Business Associate Agreement. Think of it as two tiers: your systems are local and air-gapped from us; the AI reasoning layer is cloud-based and fully BAA-covered. We sign a BAA before touching any protected health information.
I don't have a spare desktop. Is that a problem?
Not at all. We'll buy a Mac Studio for you, set it up, and keep it online 24/7 so your automations never go dark. It just needs connectivity to the systems you already use.
Do you integrate with PointClickCare / Dentrix / my software?
We run on your own logins with a human in the loop, so we don't need a vendor to grant us API access. That means we can automate software that has no open API (like PointClickCare), and nothing clinical, coded, or billed is ever submitted without your team's sign-off.
When would I hit AI usage overage?
Rarely. Usage is typically about $200/month, passed through at cost. You'd only exceed it running tens of thousands of AI actions a month, and at that volume the automation is already saving you far more than it costs.
Will we get notified if something needs our attention?
Yes. amux sends push notifications directly to your phone and email when a workflow surfaces something that needs your sign-off or hits a situation it cannot resolve on its own. You can also check in at any time from the phone app. The goal is that your inbox and phone do the alerting; you do not have to remember to check a dashboard.
Can we start with a pilot before committing?
Yes. We offer a fixed-price paid pilot: one workflow live in your office within two weeks, at a flat fee credited in full toward onboarding if you continue. You see it working before you commit to the monthly. Book a meeting and we'll scope it on the call.
