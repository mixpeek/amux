## Bottlenecks, ranked by cost

### B1. Half of main's rust runs were red, and nothing deployed for 4 hours (top)

No build ran from 16:05Z to 20:07Z. Commits from that stretch waited up to
259 minutes to go live. Most of the reds came from checks that take
seconds and could have run before the push.

Failures across the 20 red rust runs. One run can count in more than one
row:

| What failed | Runs | Caused by |

| A single e2e spec, plus a test shard in the same run | 6 | a different spec each time |

The declaration tests were: route_table 7, timestamp_units_declared 6,
rename_covers_every_session_table 6, cli_curl_timeout_guard 4,
system_jobs 3, proxy_composition 3. Each one runs in under half a second.
CI reports them 20 to 30 minutes after the push, though, and nothing ran
them before it.

harness) went red twice in 24 hours: mine in b219fbda1, and amux's in
2642d3295, which was fixed 8 minutes later in b207e9bc7.
