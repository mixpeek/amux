## Bottlenecks, ranked by cost

### B1. Goal checks are filed as your requests (top)

When a goal's email or calendar check finds something new, it wakes the
goal's worker by posting into the outcome thread as you
(`intent_items.rs` to `start_outcome` to `owner_post_to_outcome`). The
worker reads "owner asked for this in amux", and the request ledger records
a new owner request. That happened 29 times in 24h. 40 of these are open
now: 31 on security and 9 on email-connections, up to 159 hours old. They
outnumber your real open requests four to one, and the stale-request
reminder planned in AF-1334 would nag on all of them.

Card: AF-1346. The check posts as amux, still reaching the worker, and the
ledger counts only posts by a person.

### B2. amux's own notices wake goal workers

5 of the 29 wakes were caused by mail amux sent itself from your accounts:
"amux: 5 email drafts waiting for your approval" (three goals), "amux URGENT"
(twice) and "amux: reconnect hello@amux.io". A worker drafts an email, amux
mails you a notice, and the notice wakes another goal worker for a full turn.
The 5 DMARC reports that woke the email-domains goals are a fair match, so
they are left alone. Card: AF-1347.

### B3. The browser-route refusal loop continues

4,166 refusals in 24h, the fifth audit in a row. Evidence added to AF-1323.
