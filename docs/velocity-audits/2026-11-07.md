### B1. The fleet sat on a refused Claude subscription for four hours (top)

Anthropic refuses ("Claude Code can't use your Claude subscription, usually
because a payment is past due"). It held there as `current_is_best` until
you asked at 09:58 ("whats the status?", then MSG-105059 "figure out why
claude rotation didnt happen"). 7add388c6 gives three causes:
- `/usage` answered "rate limited", which the trial took as a working login.
- Idle workers' status lines repeated another account's numbers.
- `auth_lost` did not know the refusal text.

turn was refused. You found it by asking; no fleet sweep caught it first.

for this before; the 25 IndexedDB resets in one minute on your client
(client 3109dcb7) were in the log the whole time.

Cost: one reload per occurrence; unknown count before today.
