## Bottlenecks, ranked by cost

### B1. A commit with no compile still waits minutes for e2e (top)

**Corrected 2026-11-09:** the mechanism below is wrong. Activation waits
only for the 16 s `gate` workflow. The minutes are the land gate's, before
the push; `push_to_live_s` counts from the commit's committer time. See
"Push to live, 2026-11-09" in `docs/design/ci-under-a-minute.md`. The
costs and 25a are restated there.

Since a90b92b05 a dashboard or site commit goes live without a compile. In
daytime it still took minutes, because activation waits for the `rust`
workflow, and that workflow runs all 16 e2e shards on every dashboard
commit. Each shard took 280 to 530 s (94115318f, ecc956210, adfe6b6e5,
23778001d).

`push_to_live_s` for the 13 commits that skipped the compile: 549, 211,
559, 363, 330, 427, 140, 36, 543, 165, 48, 50, 44. The four under 60 s went
out overnight. The commits that compiled took 173 to 1,826 s: 4c1bf9485
waited 30 minutes, ccb4c3d91 26 and 4fb1e9e7c 22.

The land gate has already run the e2e specs that `scripts/ci-affected.mjs`
picks for the change before the commit reaches main. CI then runs the whole
suite again, and activation waits for it.

Cost: 2 to 9 minutes on every dashboard commit by day, and 22 to 30
minutes for a Rust commit under afternoon load. MSG-105242 (CI/CD under 2
minutes) is met only at night.

### B2. The demo videos took 19 hours and four check-ins from you

MSG-105405 (10:53) asked amux-homepage to remake every /demos clip. You
then sent MSG-105523 at 16:16 ("whats the status?"), MSG-105548 at 16:41
("Have any been finished?"), MSG-105552 at 16:49 and MSG-105562 at 17:04

- **No review before reporting.** The worker sent you to a clip that "had

- **No view into its own background agent.** It told you "I can't give you

  and 2682b23d6, which have the same subject, then c50960574, which
  versions the clip URLs because the CDN kept serving the old recordings.

Cost: four messages from you, your own review of unusable clips, and about
19 hours. The ping to Doron (MSG-105407) waited behind it.

## Proposals

- **25a (B1).** Let the builder activate a commit that needs no compile once
  `checks` is green and its land-gate receipt shows the affected e2e specs
  passed. The full e2e suite keeps running after the commit is live, and a
  red result reverts it. You approved "fast checks with revert-on-red" as
  SA-9 (AH-325), and it is not built yet. Expected: dashboard and site
  commits live in under 2 minutes by day as well as at night.
- **25b (B2).** Heavy media jobs (recordings, renders) declare a resource
  profile and wait in one queue, so the Mac runs one at a time without you
  asking. A background agent's output files go on its task record, so the
  worker that started it can count them.
- **25c (B3).** When a send repeats text already recorded for that lane
  within a minute, add a repeat count to the first history row instead of
  recording a new request, and log the fold. Fold the identity half into
  24e: a send with no worker identity is not `kind=human`.
- **B4** needs no proposal. It needs your answer.
- **20c** and **24b to 24e** are unchanged. 24e gets new evidence: MSG-105427
  is an `origin:api` send counted as yours.
