  the last captured frame marked stale and its age, the same way 6d998930a
  now treats the fleet listing, and log `peek_reused_last`. A 503 there

  stays 200 with `status: degraded` and the failing probe named, and a

  fleet is quiet. That is what turns a busy afternoon into a load of 49.

  the build and e2e, and refuses in seconds.
