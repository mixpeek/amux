## Added 10:05: the resource guard reads stale swap as critical

From 09:16 to 10:02, the guard refused three times to compile 26b's one unit
test ("the machine is critical (swap 99% used)"), while:
- `amux resources` reported `level ok`;
- `memory_pressure` reported "System-wide memory free percentage: 57%";
- `vm.swapusage` showed 29,291 of 29,696 MB used.

`scripts/hooks/boundary-guard.py` `classify()` makes `swap_used >=
swap_used_critical` (0.97, `boundaries.toml`) critical on its own. macOS does
not give swap back when pressure falls, so after one heavy stretch the guard
stays critical for hours on history. Every worker's builds, test suites and
browsers wait the whole time, and the two tools disagree about the same
machine.

- **26e.** Swap above the threshold counts as critical only while the kernel's
  pressure level (`kern.memorystatus_vm_pressure_level`) is at least strained.
  Swap with normal pressure is history. The guard and `amux resources` read the
  same rule. Both files are `[owner]` tier, so this needs your approval of the
  diff.
