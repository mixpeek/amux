## Patterns worth borrowing

1. **The product is the hero.** The strongest pages (Conductor, Happy, Paseo,
   Claude Code) show the real product on the first screen. A drawn mockup reads
   as a promise; a recording reads as proof.
2. **One short headline, one line under it.** Four to nine words, then a single
   sentence. Nobody explains the feature list above the fold.
3. **One primary action, one secondary.** Most pages have one button. A command
   to copy (Vibe Kanban, Warp, Cosyra) sits as a quiet line, not a third button.
4. **Few sections.** The leaders stop after the demo, three short claims and a
   way to start. Comparison tables, FAQs and long feature grids live on their
   own pages.
5. **Quiet chrome.** Headers carry three to five links and one button.

## What amux.io changed

- First screen: headline, one sentence, Cloud and Star buttons, the install
  command, a quiet "Also on iPhone and Mac" line, then the real dashboard
  recording (from the 2026-10-18 walkthrough short, trimmed to 47 seconds) with
  the phone recording beside it. On phones only the phone recording loads.
- Removed from the homepage: the badge, the hand-built mockup and its tabs, the
  runtimes strip, the 4-minute walkthrough, the feature clip, the comparison
  table, the FAQ, the move-to-Cloud note and the five-column footer. They live
  on /demos/, /walkthrough/, /compare/ and /faq/.
- Header on every page: demos, docs, pricing (guides and compare moved to the
  footer).
- Result: about 140 words instead of about 1,000, and a page 1,945px tall
  instead of 6,547px at 1440 wide.
